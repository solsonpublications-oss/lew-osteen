LEW OSTEEN — Author Biography

Lew Osteen is an award-winning writer known for his painstaking research on his historically based novels.

In researching The Joshua Trail Trilogy, he spent years in the California gold country — walking the ground, reading the records, and listening to the land where the story is set.

His work spans epic westerns, historical fiction, mystery and spy thrillers, apocalyptic prophecy fiction and suspense. He is also credited as a writer on several short films.

Catalog highlights:
- The Joshua Trail Trilogy (Six Notch Road, The Joshua Trail, A Season of Reckoning) — epic westerns
- The Apocalypse Cometh + Wake Up World! — apocalyptic prophecy fiction
- The Judgement — mystery/thriller/suspense (Orion Publishing, 2024)
- A Yellow Cotton Dress — historical novel
- An Old Familiar Rain — mystery/spy thriller
- The Pyramids of Nod — upcoming science fiction novel
